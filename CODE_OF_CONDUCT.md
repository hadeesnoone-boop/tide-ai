# 🌊 Tide Code of Conduct

*Rise together. Stay human.*

---

## Our Promise

Tide is a space where everyone belongs – regardless of age, background, identity, or experience level.

We promise to keep Tide:
- **Open** – No gatekeeping. No "not invented here."
- **Respectful** – Disagree without disrespect.
- **Safe** – No harassment, no hate, no intimidation.

---

## Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what's best for the community
- Showing empathy toward others

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private info without permission
- Any form of bigotry, hate speech, or discrimination

---

## Our Responsibilities

Maintainers of Tide can remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

But here's the truth: **we'd rather educate than ban.** If someone messes up, we talk first. If they keep messing up, they're out.

---

## Scope

This applies in all Tide spaces – GitHub repo, chat channels, forums, and any public event where Tide is present.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

- Open a private issue (label it `CODE OF CONDUCT`)
- Or email: `tide@rise.global` (coming soon)

We will respond within 48 hours.

---

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
1. A warning
2. Temporary ban
3. Permanent ban

No one is above the Code – not even the first contributors.
