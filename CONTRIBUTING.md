## How to Contribute

### 1. Find your current

- **Code** – Python, decentralization, privacy tools
- **Docs** – Make Tide understandable for everyone
- **Design** – Logo, UI concepts, visual identity
- **Ideas** – Fresh perspectives on alignment, governance, or features
- **Community** – Help others find Tide and feel welcome

No contribution is too small. The ocean is made of drops.
